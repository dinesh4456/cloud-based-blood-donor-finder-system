How BloodFinder Works

A seamless 3-step bridge between voluntary donors and patients.

1. Register as Donor

Sign up with your blood group, contact number, and city. Toggle your availability status anytime with one tap.

2. Search or Post Request

Search available donors in your area or submit an urgent emergency blood request with hospital details.

3. Connect & Save Lives

Donors receive immediate notification to accept the request, coordinating with the hospital for life-saving transfusion.
